# Redesign audit

## Finding format

For each material issue record:

- Evidence: what is observably happening and where
- Impact: the user, brand, accessibility, or implementation consequence
- Cause: the underlying content, layout, token, component, or behavior problem
- Recommendation: a bounded corrective direction
- Preservation constraint: what must continue to work
- Verification: how the result will be compared

Classify severity by consequence, not visual annoyance: blocking, high, medium, or polish.

## Preserve equity

Identify recognizable assets, vocabulary, colors, typography, interaction conventions, and layouts. Keep the parts that carry trust or product memory unless the brief explicitly rejects them.

## Compare the full state space

Review representative routes and widths plus focus, hover, active, disabled, loading, error, empty, expanded, authenticated, and long-content states that actually exist. Use screenshots when possible and verify function separately from appearance.
