# Review scorecard

## Severity

- **Blocking:** prevents a critical task, access, comprehension, or safe release.
- **High:** materially harms a common task, accessibility, brand trust, or responsive use.
- **Medium:** noticeable inconsistency or friction with a bounded impact.
- **Polish:** improvement that does not obstruct use or system coherence.

## Coverage

- Purpose and content hierarchy
- Navigation and task completion
- Typography and reading comfort
- Semantic and visual color behavior
- Layout rhythm and responsive recomposition
- Component consistency and complete states
- Keyboard, focus, screen-reader, pointer, zoom, and reduced-motion behavior
- Image and media loading, crop, description, and fallback
- Motion causality and interruption
- Long, empty, loading, success, error, and disabled states
- Performance symptoms and layout stability

## Evidence boundary

State what was actually inspected. A source review can identify structural risks; a rendered review can identify visual behavior; interaction and assistive-technology checks establish operational evidence. Do not imply broader coverage than the review performed.
