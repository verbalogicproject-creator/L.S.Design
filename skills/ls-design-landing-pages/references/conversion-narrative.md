# Conversion narrative

## Narrative ingredients

Select and order only the ingredients supported by the product:

- Recognition of the audience's situation
- A precise value proposition
- Product or service demonstration
- Credible proof and provenance
- Explanation of how it works
- Objection handling
- Comparison or qualification
- Reassurance about risk, privacy, support, or setup
- A clear next action

The hero does not need to explain everything. It must create correct expectations and earn the next scroll or action.

## Conversion integrity

Use evidence at its real strength. Label simulations and illustrative visuals. Connect claims to sources when available. If proof is missing, reserve an explicitly marked content slot for the owner to complete instead of inventing content.

## Visual pacing

Alternate modes because the story changes: copy, product image, interactive demonstration, comparison, testimonial, or concise data. Avoid alternating left-right blocks mechanically. On small screens, reorder each section to preserve the argument rather than its desktop symmetry.
