# Direction families

Use these as lenses, not presets.

## Editorial restraint

Type-led hierarchy, deliberate measure, strong cropping, quiet surfaces, and disciplined alignment. Distinction comes from proportion and pacing.

## Precise technical

Structured information, calibrated density, exact labels, diagrams, controlled contrast, and motion tied to system state. Avoid decorative terminal clichés unless authentic to the product.

## Tactile human

Material imagery, warm color relationships, irregular but controlled composition, generous reading comfort, and motion with physical continuity.

## Cinematic spatial

Depth, sequence, framing, light, and atmospheric media create focus. Preserve immediate comprehension, DOM content, and a lightweight fallback.

## Cultural expressive

Distinctive typography, image language, rhythm, and symbols grounded in a real cultural or brand context. Avoid superficial borrowing.

For any direction, specify what changes and what remains stable. Test the direction against ordinary states, not only a hero composition.
