# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in L.S.Design to be open, respectful, and useful. Everyone may contribute without harassment based on identity, background, experience, appearance, disability, nationality, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Give context for design preferences and technical claims.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Credit sources and other contributors.
- Protect private information and unpublished vulnerability details.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language
- Sexual attention or imagery in community spaces
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument
- Plagiarism or false attribution
- Encouraging unsafe or unauthorized actions

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Include relevant links or screenshots when possible. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as private as practical.

The maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban a participant according to the seriousness and pattern of the behaviour.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not allowed.
